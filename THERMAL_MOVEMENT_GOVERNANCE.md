# Thermal Movement Governance

## Core Claim

Heat is not merely waste.

Heat is governed movement.

A device action proposes a thermal consequence. The field resolves whether that heat may remain, route, partially bind, or refuse before thermal violation.

---

## Non-Reduction Rule

Thermal movement governance is not ordinary heat-management software.

It is not:

```text
dashboard
alarm
post-event thermal log
basic cooling control
temperature report
single-device throttle
```

It is a lawful movement-governance surface applied to thermodynamic consequence:

```text
what can remain
what must route
what may partially bind
what must refuse before thermal breach
```

---

## Governing Sentence

```text
Waste heat is not waste when the basin can lawfully receive it.
Heat is refused only when the field cannot carry the movement.
```

---

## Thermal Action Object

A heat-producing action is incomplete until it declares its proposed thermal burden.

```text
Delta Q_heat
```

The proposed heat is not an after-effect.

It is part of the movement object.

A device is not allowed to run merely because it requests action.

The thermal field must resolve standing first.

---

## Thermal Capacity Field

Remaining lawful capacity is expressed as:

```text
Phi(x) = C(x) - M(x)
```

In thermal application:

```text
Phi(x) = field headroom
C(x) = available thermal capacity
M(x) = carried thermal burden / pressure
```

A thermal movement is lawful only if the field can carry, route, partially admit, or refuse the proposed heat consequence before violation.

---

## Thermal Admissibility Region

The lawful continuation region remains:

```text
R = { z | A(z) >= 0, Phi(z) >= 0, U(z) = 1 }
```

For thermal movement:

```text
A(z) = admissibility of proposed thermal movement
Phi(z) = remaining thermal headroom
U(z) = authority-validity / operating standing
```

A heat-producing device may run only to the degree that thermal standing remains valid.

---

## Outcomes

Thermal movement introduces an applied outcome class:

```text
ALLOW
PARTIAL
REFUSE
```

Where:

```text
ALLOW   = full proposed thermal movement can be carried
PARTIAL = only a bounded lawful fraction can be carried
REFUSE  = no lawful thermal capacity remains
```

`PARTIAL` is not degraded logging.

`PARTIAL` is bounded lawful continuation under field capacity.

---

## Partial Thermodynamic Execution

If full thermal movement cannot hold, the system may bind only the lawful fraction.

```text
0 < allowed_heat < proposed_heat
```

A partial decision must include:

```text
proposed_heat
allowed_heat
refused_heat
sink_assignment
remaining_capacity
receipt / witness object
```

Partial admission is valid only when the lawful fraction is explicitly bounded and receipted.

Unbounded partial execution is projection error.

---

## Heat Sinks as Lawful Transfer Basins

A heat sink is a lawful transfer basin with finite absorption capacity.

Examples:

```text
radiator
storage
building
process load
district heat path
battery thermal region
AI compute cooling field
```

A sink may receive heat only to its remaining capacity.

Local overflow is not safety.

If no basin can hold or receive the heat, the movement must refuse before violation.

---

## Conservation as Routing

Heat cannot disappear from the proof surface.

It must be accounted as:

```text
local retention
sink transfer
partial binding
refusal
```

Conservation becomes operational routing, not retrospective explanation.

A governed thermal system records:

```text
proposed heat
```

## Field-Level Governance

Thermal admissibility cannot be decided by a device in isolation.

The field contains:

```text
regions
sinks
shared capacity
prior commitments
routing paths
changed-condition pressure
continuity state
```

A single device can consume headroom assumed by another.

Therefore, thermal standing must be resolved at field level.

## Doctrine

Heat-producing motion does not have standing merely because the device can execute.

It has standing only if the thermal field can lawfully carry, route, partially admit, or refuse the proposed heat consequence.

```text
Heat is governed movement.
```
