# Standing Decay Theorem

## Core Claim

Standing is not permanent.

A movement that had lawful standing at one point may lose standing before consequence status is admitted.

Permission granted earlier does not automatically survive changed conditions.

---

## Why This Matters

Many systems inherit prior approval forward as if standing were static.

That is a reduction.

A movement may begin with valid authority, fresh evidence, intact lineage, and sufficient capacity, then lose standing before consequence binds.

The proof question is not:

```text
Was the movement once approved?
```

The proof question is:

```text
Does the movement still have standing at the boundary where consequence would bind?
```

---

## Formal Context

The lawful region of continuation is:

```text
R = { z | A(z) >= 0, Phi(z) >= 0, U(z) = 1 }
```

Standing survives only while the movement remains inside the lawful region across material changes.

If any required standing dimension decays, the movement must be re-evaluated.

---

## Standing Decay Conditions

Standing may decay when:

```text
authority changes
evidence goes stale
scope narrows
lineage breaks
capacity is consumed
transfer path closes
route standing fails
witness continuity is lost
law version changes
consequence exposure increases
```

These are not external notes.

They are material standing changes.

---

## Theorem

If a movement `M` previously satisfied lawful continuation, but a material standing dimension changes before consequence status is admitted, then prior standing cannot be inherited without re-evaluation.

```text
standing(M, t0) = valid
changed_condition(t1) = true
```

does not imply:

```text
standing(M, t1) = valid
```

The movement must be resolved again against current field conditions.

---

## Non-Inheritance Rule

Prior approval is not standing.

Prior standing is not current standing.

Current standing must be reconstructed at the bind boundary.

```text
standing_t0 != standing_t1 unless continuity is proven
```

---

## Relation to Replay

Legitimacy replay must preserve standing-change history.

A replay that reconstructs only the original approval but not the later standing decay is execution replay only.

Required replay fields include:

```text
original standing basis
changed condition
standing decay reason
re-evaluation result
kernel decision after change
protected effect status
```

---

## Relation to Movement Governance

The movement-governance proof surface requires showing what happens when conditions change.

If authority changes, evidence goes stale, scope narrows, or continuity breaks, standing must be re-evaluated.

If standing collapses, movement cannot acquire consequence status.

---

## Doctrine

Standing is live.

Approval is historical.

```text
A movement may only bind under current standing, not inherited assumption.
```
